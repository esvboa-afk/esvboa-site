About ESVBOA

An approved MHSAA association, serving Greater Metro Detroit since 1980.

Who we are

The East Side Volleyball Officials Association has served the Greater Metropolitan Detroit volleyball community since 1980. We educate, develop, and train volleyball officials who work under Michigan High School Athletic Association (MHSAA) regulations and the National Federation of State High School Associations (NFHS) rules.

Our training staff runs clinics and events for members.

New to officiating?

You do not need experience. We walk you through registering with the MHSAA, joining ESVBOA, and getting your first matches.
