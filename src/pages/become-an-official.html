Become an official

Five steps from first interest to your first match.

  1. Get your free MHSAA membership voucher

    If you have never registered with the MHSAA, email {{site.email}} for a voucher for a free MHSAA membership.

    Do this first. The voucher cannot be used after you have registered.

  2. Register with the MHSAA

    Registration opens after June 1 on the MHSAA website. When they are available, take these exams:

    • Officials Guidebook
    • Principles of Officiating
    • Health & Safety
    • Sport-specific exam (volleyball)

    Then attend the virtual rules meetings, on your own time: the general officials meeting and the volleyball meeting. The MHSAA mails your rule book after you register.

    The Officials Guidebook (PDF) explains how officiating works under the MHSAA.

  3. Join ESVBOA

    We provide training and mentoring for officials across Metro Detroit. Registration begins in June, with a training clinic in mid-August.

    2026/27 membership form

  4. Get the right uniform and equipment

    The uniform and equipment guide lists what you need before your first match.

  5. Contact local assigners

    Assigners fill the officiating needs of the schools they represent, and they give officials their matches. Use the assigner list to reach them.

New officials get extra support

Orientations for new officials are held before various events. If you are new or have under three years of experience, start with the 2026-27 new officials presentation, then check the events page for the next orientation.
