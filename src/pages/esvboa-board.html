ESVBOA board

The volunteers who run the association.

Officers

  • Grant Harris

    President

    Retired General Motors engineering group manager with 22 years of officiating experience. Elected president in 2024 after seven years on the board. Also coaches varsity volleyball at Algonac High School.

    “KISS—Keep it simple, stupid!”

  • Mick Hashley

    Vice President

    Volleyball official for more than 15 years. Has served as Recruiting and Mentorship Coordinator since the role was created seven years ago. Officiated the MHSAA Division 2 State Finals in 2021.

    “It’s not about us; it’s about the game!”

  • Samantha Jay

    Secretary

    Port Huron native and Sales Manager at the St. Clair Inn. Began officiating in 2020, has worked an MHSAA state final, and won a USAV Junior National class award in 2023. Serving her second term as secretary.

  • PB

    Perry Brunetti

    Treasurer

    Royal Oak resident and MHSAA official since 2001, with about 20 seasons in volleyball. Retired I.T. infrastructure engineer from General Motors. Serving his first term as treasurer.

Directors and coordinators

  • Augusto “Gus” Dumlao

    Director of Training

    Officiating since 2009 and a USA Volleyball Junior National Referee. Director of Training since 2020, and helped develop the pre-season clinics and in-season meetings.

  • KL

    Kelly Lemanski

    Membership Recruitment Coordinator

    Questions about joining? Kelly’s contact details are on the About page.

At large

  • Mike Roch

    At large

    Junior National volleyball official with 25 years of experience. Former college player and six-year coach. Studied at St. Clair College and the University of Windsor.

  • Kourtney “K.T.” Thompson

    At large

    Retired Michigan educator with more than 30 years in the classroom. Now officiates high school and college volleyball and baseball.

About the board

Board members are volunteers. Apart from being association members, they are independent contractors and not employees of ESVBOA.
